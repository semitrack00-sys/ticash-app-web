FLUPFLAP PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026. This policy explains how the FlupFlap mobile recharge experience by TiCash-App handles information used to provide accounts, recharge, payments, transaction history and support.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use FlupFlap, the service may process information you provide directly and information created by the service.

  • Account information such as first name, last name, phone number, email address, password-derived authentication data and optional billing country.
  • Recharge information such as destination country, recipient mobile number, operator, selected airtime/data/bundle product, quote, provider, transaction status and timestamps.
  • Saved recipient information when you choose to save recipients.
  • Payment-related references and status needed to coordinate checkout. FlupFlap does not expose payment-provider secret keys to the browser.
  • Security, session, audit and operational records needed to protect accounts, investigate failures and reconcile transactions.
  • Support messages you choose to send to the official support address.

2. How information is used

  • Create and secure FlupFlap accounts and sessions.
  • Detect supported mobile operators and retrieve provider-backed recharge products.
  • Create quotes, process checkout, submit eligible recharge fulfillment and display transaction status/history.
  • Prevent duplicate or unauthorized transactions and enforce idempotency and account ownership.
  • Investigate failed, pending, cancelled, refunded or disputed transactions.
  • Provide customer support, security monitoring, audit logging and service reliability.

3. Payments and recharge providers

FlupFlap relies on external services to complete parts of a transaction. Payment checkout is coordinated with the configured payment provider, and recharge fulfillment may use configured providers such as DT One, Reloadly or Ding when available. Information necessary to perform the selected service may be sent to those providers under the transaction flow.

Provider availability varies by country, operator and product. FlupFlap does not invent operator plans, recharge values, bundle allowances or provider status.

4. Cookies, sessions and account security

FlupFlap uses authentication/session mechanisms needed to keep accounts signed in securely. Access credentials and provider secrets are not intentionally placed in public page content. Do not share your password or verification information with anyone claiming to be support.

5. Transaction history and deletion

Customers may be able to remove certain safely cancelled transactions from their visible FlupFlap history. That action does not necessarily erase the underlying financial, audit or reconciliation record retained by TiCash-App for operational integrity, security and transaction review.

6. Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the relevant function, including payment processing, recharge fulfillment, hosting, security and support. FlupFlap does not expose provider credentials, payment secrets or internal administrative records to customers.

7. Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain transaction and audit records, investigate security or payment issues, resolve disputes and meet applicable business or legal obligations. Different categories may have different retention periods.

8. Your choices

You can choose whether to create a permanent account or use guest access where available. You can review transaction history, manage saved recipients where supported, and contact support regarding account or privacy questions. Some transaction and audit records may need to remain after a customer-facing item is removed.

9. Security

FlupFlap uses technical and operational controls intended to reduce unauthorized access, including authenticated ownership checks, server-authoritative transaction state, audit logging and restricted access to provider/payment credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as FlupFlap changes. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are published.
