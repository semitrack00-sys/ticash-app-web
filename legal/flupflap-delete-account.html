FLUPFLAP ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your FlupFlap account

Request deletion of your account and associated personal data. You can use this page without signing in or reinstalling the app.

Send your deletion request

  1. Email contact@ticash-app.com from the email address registered to your FlupFlap account.
  2. Use the subject “FlupFlap account deletion request” and state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
  3. If you cannot access your registered email, explain this so support can help verify your request.

Write deletion request

Opening your email app does not send a request. Review and send it yourself. If no email app opens, copy the address above and send the message from your email service.

Do not include passwords, payment card details or identity documents in your email.

What happens next?

Support reviews and verifies the request before processing deletion. Ask support to stop any recurring recharges as part of your request. Support can explain verification steps, expected handling time and any records that need to be retained.

The request covers your FlupFlap account and its associated personal data, including profile and saved-recipient information. Certain transaction, security, audit or reconciliation records may need to be retained for legitimate reasons, including fraud prevention, disputes or applicable obligations. See our privacy policy and contact support for applicable retention details.

Signing out or removing a receipt from visible history does not delete your account. This page opens an email request; it does not automatically erase your data.
