Pending transactions

Account & security

Use Sign in, Create account or Forgot password from the FlupFlap access page. Never send passwords, payment credentials or identity documents by ordinary email.

💳

Payments

Payment status is controlled by the TiCash backend and payment provider. Do not retry a charge until the transaction status is clear.
